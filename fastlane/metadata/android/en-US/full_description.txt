OpenJournal can help you keep track of your substance use and give you insights on your consumption patterns. It is an open-source fork of the PsychonautWiki Journal app, focused on harm reduction and user privacy.

The substance info comes from PsychonautWiki with some additional info from TripSit and saferparty.ch.

The app is designed to work fully offline. The data you enter in the app never leaves your device.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• <b>100% Free & Open Source:</b> The core of this app will always remain free and accessible to everyone.
• <b>No Arbitrary Limits:</b> Track as many active substances as you need simultaneously.
• <b>Substance Information:</b> Access to verified data from PsychonautWiki.
• <b>Advanced Secure Logging:</b> Document experiences, dosages, and effects in a privacy-friendly environment.
• <b>Harm Reduction Tools:</b> Built-in tools for risk minimization (e.g., interactions checker, dosage calculators).

OpenJournal is a fork of the original PsychonautWiki Journal, relicensed to ensure continued open access and community-driven development.
